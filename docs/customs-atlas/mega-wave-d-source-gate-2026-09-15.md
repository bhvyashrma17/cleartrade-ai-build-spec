### Reuse disposition

The rapid gate did not treat public access as commercial permission. Reuse is provisionally
favourable only for Sri Lanka (the publishing directorate calls the tariff guide public-domain),
Brazil (gov.br licence plus separately reusable official acts/facts), and Côte d’Ivoire/China to
the limited extent faithful official enactments and raw tariff facts can be separated from an
original compilation. Singapore's general open-data licence does not attach to its Customs tariff;
the UAE's platform licence still needs dataset attachment; and Namibia's official facts must be
separated from Customs Connect software/presentation. For every other roster member (`TH`, `BN`,
`KH`, `LA`, `MN`, `SA`, `QA`, `KW`, `LB`, `EC`, `BO`, `GT`, `SV`, `HN`, `NI`, `BZ`, `GH`, `SN`,
`TZ`, `UG`, `RW`, `ZM`, `BW`), reuse remains unresolved and would require either source-specific
commercial terms, an applicable official-text/factual-data exclusion, or written authority
permission before activation. It is not labelled `BLOCKED_RIGHTS` because an independent source,
machine-acquisition or tariff-completeness blocker already prevents implementation and the wave's
stop condition forbids spending a second investigation budget on a non-controlling issue.

## Family result

No provider family was built because no country reached `PASS`. Building a reader without a
publishable member would be the theoretical abstraction prohibited by the wave. The reusable
architectures identified for the next focused work are:

| Family | Candidates | What is genuinely reusable | What remains national |
|---|---|---|---|
| `STRUCTURED_TABLE` | CN, BR | Typed table/rate reader and immutable file manifests | Remedies, quotas, preferences, effectivity and update feeds |
| `OFFICIAL_PDF_TABLE` | LK, CI, ZM | Coordinate extraction, layout fingerprinting, row-count and fail-closed rate parsing | Columns, formulas, amendment reconciliation and preference semantics |
| `BASELINE_PLUS_AMENDMENTS` | TH, BN, BO, SV, TZ, UG, RW, ZM | Ordered immutable manifests with ADD/REPLACE/SUSPEND/EXPIRE | Authority-specific scope, conditions, quota balance and precedence |
| `REGIONAL_BASE_PLUS_NATIONAL_OVERLAY` | AE/SA/QA/KW; GT/SV/HN/NI; GH/CI/SN; TZ/UG/RW; BW/NA | GCC/SAC/ECOWAS/EAC/SACU nomenclature and baseline parsing | Every destination’s executable rates, exceptions, taxes, remedies and updates |
| `DOCUMENTED_API` | BR only | Existing authority-release, cache and replay infrastructure | TTCE credentials and Brazil-specific treatment semantics |

ASEAN/AHTN supplies nomenclature reuse, not a common external tariff. GCC, SIECA, ECOWAS and EAC
likewise do not eliminate national execution. SACU is the one new family with real shared rate
execution, but its multi-schedule amendments and member overlays make it an individual
investigation rather than a safe bulk activation.

## Publication and environment result

- No tariff source passed, so no `AuthorityRelease`, snapshot, ingest job or job attempt was
  created. No canary can honestly exist for a blocked source.
- No staging database mutation, provider ingestion, canary or deployment was required. The required
  storage validation wrote one isolated verification object to the staging bucket, verified it,
  and deleted it successfully; the runtime blocker registry is the only application-code change.
- Production was not accessed, deployed to, or mutated.
- Tariff and regulatory completeness remain separate: several candidates could tolerate unknown
  permits, but every blocker above concerns tariff execution, acquisition, replay or a
  tariff-affecting overlay.
