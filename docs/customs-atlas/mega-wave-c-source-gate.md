## Sixteen-gate disposition

For every destination above, the authority and current source were identified (gates 1–2). Gates
3–6 were evaluated independently: interface availability, artifact-specific reuse, currency through
September 2026, and a reproducible amendment mechanism. Regional sources were accepted only as
leads; gates 7–10 require the national overlay chain and distinct parsing of ad-valorem,
specific/mixed/compound and additional duties. No AVE conversion is permitted.

India preference existence, destination-level qualification, concessions and rules of origin were
kept separate (gates 11–13). A preference remains unknown unless the national concession schedule
and operative RoO are retained together. Gates 14–16 require UTC date-only effectiveness,
source-level provenance, and immutable retained bytes with per-file SHA-256 plus a release checksum.
None of the 20 candidates cleared all sixteen gates, so no provider, release or evidence snapshot was
created.

## Runtime behavior and operational count

Each country has a distinct entry in `UNCONFIGURED_MARKET_ACCESS_SOURCES`. A valid ingest request for
one of the 20 completes with `provider_state=provider_unconfigured_or_license_pending`,
`snapshot_id=null`, its exact authority, reason, references and unblock condition. It includes no
tariff percentage, no zero, no provider evidence and no database write. Concurrent processing is
covered so one blocker cannot stop the other nineteen.

Operational destinations remain **39**. Mega-Wave D is out of scope and was not begun.
